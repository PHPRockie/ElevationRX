How coaches get invited to a gym
1
Admin creates the gym in Supabase — adds a row to the gyms table (name, country)
2
Admin invites coach by email via Supabase dashboard → Supabase sends invite email
3
Admin adds a row to coaches table with the coach's auth.uid() and their gym's gym_id
4
Coach clicks the invite link, sets their password, and lands on the ElevationRx login page
✓
App reads their gym — on login, looks up coaches by auth.uid() → loads gym name → shows it in the sidebar
